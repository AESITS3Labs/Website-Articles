AESIT — Tysons Corner, Virginia

AESIT emerged at the hinge between eras — when coordination shifted from hand‑aligned overlays to the first digital twin environments. Born in that metamorphosis, AESIT preserved the practices that guided earlier generations while integrating the emerging digital toolsets that would transform the built environment.

A practice built to bridge eras, technologies, and the cultures that shaped them — carrying each forward into smarter, more enduring futures.

Classified Federal Environment — United States

Multi-disciplinary architecture and engineering support for a high-consequence federal environment — structural hardening, complex telecommunications pathways, and early cyber security considerations.

Proved that physical design, operational continuity, and secure protection must be co-engineered as one unified system.

AESIT did not invent CRELI™ or Environmentsmithing™ in 2026. Instead, we formalized and named the intelligence and governance architecture that had been evolving across decades of design, operations, and protection practice.
