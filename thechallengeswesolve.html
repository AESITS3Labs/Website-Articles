The Sovereignty Principle

Intelligence belongs to the asset —
never to the vendor.

Design intelligence, operational telemetry, protection records, lifecycle memory — the owner must retain full control of all four. Not licensed access. Not a dashboard on loan. Ownership, structurally guaranteed.

Sovereignty is the guarantee.

Not a policy that can be revised, and not a feature that can be deprecated. It is the condition every other obligation depends on — because knowledge that can be taken away was never really yours to begin with.

That is why the doctrine starts and ends with the owner — not the platform, not the cloud, and not us.

The Central Thesis

A building's intelligence should not degrade. It should compound.

Every operation, every decision, every insight should strengthen the building's future — not merely sustain its present. That is the challenge every obligation above exists to solve.
